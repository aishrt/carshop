# NZ Peer-to-Peer Car Rental Marketplace: Project Milestones

**Prepared:** September 2026
**Technology:** React website · Node.js API · MongoDB database · Hosting on AWS (Sydney)
**Total duration:** 30 days

---

## Summary

| Phase | Name | Days | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design & Foundation | Days 1–5 | Approved design for the key pages, project set up, sign-up and login working with verification emails |
| 2 | Core Marketplace | Days 6–15 | Guests can search, book and pay. Hosts can list vehicles. Booking emails are sent. |
| 3 | Dashboards, Trust & Operations | Days 16–24 | Guest, Host and Admin dashboards, messaging, reviews, verification, vehicle handover and incident reporting |
| 4 | Testing, Launch & Handover | Days 25–30 | Fully tested, live on your domain, with documentation and training |

---

## Phase 1: Design & Foundation (Days 1–5)

**What we will do**
- Day 1: confirm scope, business rules (fees, cancellation policy, deposits) and third-party accounts.
- Create a professional, luxurious brand look and feel: colours, fonts, photography and animation style.
- Design the key pages for mobile and desktop: Home, Search, Vehicle listing, Checkout and Host dashboard.
- Build a clickable prototype of the key animations (homepage, opening a car, search filters and checkout), so you can see how the site moves before we build it.
- Set up the code, the database, and the staging (test) environment.
- Build account sign-up, login, email verification and password reset, with emails sent.

**You will receive**
- Design style guide, key page designs and the animation prototype
- A staging link where you can create an account and receive a verification email

**Client sign-off (Day 5):** approval of the designs and animations.

---

## Phase 2: Core Marketplace (Days 6–15)

**What we will do**
- Homepage with location search (NZ autocomplete), dates and featured vehicles.
- Search results with vehicle cards and all filters (price, type, seats, EV/hybrid, delivery, instant booking and more).
- Vehicle listing page with photo gallery, specifications, WOF and registration info, pricing and a sticky "Book" button on mobile.
- Host onboarding in 6 steps: vehicle details, documents, photos, pricing, availability, and pickup/delivery.
- Availability calendar that prevents double-booking.
- Booking flow with a clear price breakdown in NZD (rental, delivery, service fee, protection, GST).
- Secure online payments (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
- Automatic emails: booking request, booking confirmed, payment receipt, cancellation.
- Public pages: How It Works, Become a Host, Safety, Insurance, FAQs, About, Contact and the legal pages.
- Smooth, fast animations throughout: cinematic homepage, page transitions, photo gallery, search filters and booking steps.

**You will receive**
- A working staging site where a test Guest can find a car and book it, and a test Host can list a car and receive the booking.

**Client sign-off (Day 15):** complete an end-to-end test booking on staging.

---

## Phase 3: Dashboards, Trust & Operations (Days 16–24)

**What we will do**
- **Guest dashboard:** trips (upcoming, current, completed, cancelled), receipts, saved cars, payment methods, licence verification, reviews and notifications.
- **Host dashboard:** my vehicles, bookings, calendar, earnings reports (today, week, month, lifetime), payouts and document reminders.
- **Admin dashboard:** users, Host and vehicle approvals, bookings, refunds, incidents, fee settings, FAQ and homepage content, basic reports and audit log.
- **Messaging:** chat for each booking, with photo attachments and automated pickup and return reminders.
- **Identity and driver licence verification.**
- **Digital vehicle handover:** timestamped check-in and check-out photos, odometer and fuel readings, damage notes.
- **Damage and incident reporting**, with case numbers and a full history.
- **Two-way reviews and ratings**, with moderation.
- **Design polish:** a final pass over every screen to refine animations, spacing and details on real phones.
- **Host payouts, refunds and cancellation fees.**
- **Notifications** by email and in-app, plus SMS for pickup and return reminders.

**You will receive**
- All three dashboards working on staging, with the complete trip lifecycle: book, pick up, return, review and payout.

**Client sign-off (Day 24):** review and approve the dashboards and operational workflows.

---

## Phase 4: Testing, Launch & Handover (Days 25–30)

**What we will do**
- Testing on mobile and desktop, and on Chrome, Safari and Edge.
- Security review and speed optimisation. Target: a Google Lighthouse score of 90 or more on mobile, and smooth animations on mid-range phones.
- SEO setup: page titles, meta descriptions, structured data, sitemap, landing pages for 5 main destinations (Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Queenstown, Rotorua), and Google Search Console.
- Analytics and conversion tracking.
- Email domain setup so emails land in inboxes, not spam.
- Deploy to production on your domain, with HTTPS, automated backups and error monitoring.
- User acceptance testing (UAT) with your team, then bug fixes.
- Admin training session and written documentation.

**You will receive**
- A live website on your domain
- Admin guide and technical documentation
- Access to source code and all hosting accounts

**Client sign-off (Day 30):** go-live approval.

**After launch:** 30 days of support for bug fixes.

## To deliver in 30 days, we need from you

- **By Day 1:** business name, logo and domain name (with access to its DNS settings), plus decisions on fees, cancellation policy and security deposits
- **By Day 2:** accounts for hosting (AWS), the payment provider (Stripe), email and SMS services (we will guide you through setting them up)
- **By Day 15:** insurance and protection details from your insurance partner
- **By Day 24:** legal text (Terms, Privacy, Host and Guest Agreements), reviewed by your legal adviser
- **Feedback within 24 hours** at each review point. Any delay moves the launch date by the same number of days.

## Scope for the 30-day plan

To meet the 30-day timeline:
- **Design:** designs are prepared for the key pages. The remaining pages follow the same design system directly in the build, without a separate design for each one.
- **Reports:** the admin area includes essential reports with CSV export. Advanced analytics come later.

## Not included (future phases)

- **Search and notifications:** map-based search and mobile push notifications.
- **Apps:** native iOS and Android apps.
- **Growth features:** dynamic pricing, and referral and loyalty programmes.
- **Business tools:** corporate accounts, fleet tools and advanced fraud detection.

The system is built so these can be added later without rebuilding.
