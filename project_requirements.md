NZ Peer-to-Peer Car Rental Marketplace
Website Specification
Turo-style marketplace concept adapted for New Zealand
Prepared for website design and development
September 2026
 
1. Project Overview
Build a two-sided online marketplace connecting vehicle owners (Hosts) with people looking to rent vehicles (Guests) across New Zealand. The user experience should be simple, modern and mobile-first, taking inspiration from the convenience of established peer-to-peer rental platforms while using completely original branding, wording and visual design.
Core marketplace:
•	Hosts list their vehicles and set pricing, availability and pickup/delivery options.
•	Guests search, compare, book and pay for vehicles online.
•	The platform manages verification, bookings, payments, messaging, vehicle handover records, reviews and support.
•	All consumer-facing prices should default to NZD and the platform should be designed around New Zealand users and operating requirements.
2. Primary User Types
•	Guest / Renter – searches for and books a vehicle.
•	Host / Vehicle Owner – lists and manages one or more vehicles.
•	Administrator – manages the marketplace, users, vehicles, bookings, payments, disputes and platform settings.
•	Support / Operations Staff – handles verification, customer support, incidents and booking issues.

29. Important Design Direction
•	Premium but approachable.
•	Clean NZ-focused visual identity.
•	Trust should be visible throughout the booking journey.
•	Photography should be a major part of the design.
•	Avoid clutter and excessive text.
•	Make pricing transparent.
•	Make the primary action obvious on every important page.
•	Design mobile-first before expanding to desktop.
•	Use original branding and UI rather than copying another platform.
